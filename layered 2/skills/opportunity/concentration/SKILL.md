# Single-stock concentration

Follows the parent `opportunity` skill.

## What to establish first

Name the stock, its share of the household and the amount above the limit, all from
`opportunity_facts`. Then find out how the client relates to the stock before proposing
anything: an employer's stock, an inheritance and a winning pick are three different
conversations.

## Judgment

- A position just over the limit with a large gain may be better phased over several tax
  years than trimmed at once. Say what each pace costs in tax and in risk carried.
- If the tool says the client is an insider, the conversation is about timing and
  pre-clearance before it is about how much to sell.
- If this household is also flagged for the Advisory program, mention that the two are
  connected, and let the advisor choose which to work first.
- Do not describe holding the position as wrong. Describe what it exposes the client to.
