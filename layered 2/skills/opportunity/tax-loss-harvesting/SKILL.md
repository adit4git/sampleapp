# Tax-loss harvesting

Follows the parent `opportunity` skill.

## Judgment

- Losses are only useful against gains. Ask whether the client has realized gains this year
  or expects to, before treating this as urgent.
- The client stays invested: a harvested position is replaced with something similar, not
  identical. Say what the replacement changes, if anything.
- If the same household has large embedded gains elsewhere, connect the two: losses
  harvested now make room to reduce a concentrated or overweight position.
- Tax outcomes depend on the client's whole situation. Suggest they confirm with their tax
  advisor.
